# 🏥 Hospital Patient Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Hospital Patient Analysis Dashboard** is a Power BI-based data analytics project developed to analyze hospital patient data and generate meaningful healthcare insights.

The dashboard transforms patient records into interactive visualizations that help understand patient demographics, disease patterns, readmission trends, waiting time, length of stay, hospital ward activity, and hospital operations.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Hospitals generate a large amount of patient data, including patient details, medical information, admission and discharge information, and hospital service data.

Managing and analyzing this information manually can be time-consuming and makes it difficult to quickly identify important trends and patterns.

This project aims to analyze hospital patient data efficiently and present the results through an interactive dashboard to support better understanding of patient and hospital-related patterns.

---

## 💡 Solution

A **Power BI interactive dashboard** was developed to analyze hospital patient records using data visualization and DAX calculations.

The dashboard provides multiple analytical views covering:

- Patient demographics
- Disease distribution
- Readmission patterns
- Waiting time
- Length of stay
- Hospital ward activity
- Doctor specialties
- Bed utilization
- Admission trends

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. 🏥 Hospital Overview

Provides an overall view of hospital patient activity.

**Key metrics and analysis:**
- Total Patients
- Readmitted Patients
- Average Length of Stay
- Average Waiting Time
- Patients by Age Group
- Patients by Gender
- Patients by Hospital Ward
- Admissions Over Time

---

### 2. 🧑‍⚕️ Patient & Disease Analysis

Analyzes patient demographics and disease patterns.

**Key analysis:**
- Top Diseases by Patient Count
- Patients by Gender
- Average Length of Stay by Disease
- Diseases by Age Group
- Diseases by Gender
- Diseases by Hospital Ward

---

### 3. ⚠️ High-Risk & Readmission Analysis

Focuses on readmission patterns within the available patient data.

**Key metrics and analysis:**
- Readmission Rate
- Readmitted Patients
- Non-Readmitted Patients
- Readmitted Patients by Disease
- Readmitted Patients by Age Group
- Readmitted Patients by Hospital Ward
- Readmission Trend Over Time

---

### 4. 📈 Hospital Operations & Management

Provides operational insights using available hospital data.

**Key analysis:**
- Average Waiting Time by Department
- Average Length of Stay by Hospital Ward
- Average Occupied Beds by Hospital Ward
- Average Length of Stay by Disease
- Patients by Doctor Specialty
- Average Bed Utilization
